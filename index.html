    About this list

    Each plant has four reference photos. Hover over a plant to see a second photo, or open it to browse all four; select a photo to view it full screen. Photos load from the original websites and require an internet connection. Each photo includes its source. Full aquascapes show settings whose plant lists include the featured plant.

    Prices are in AUD as listed by Australian stores in early October 2026, including any sale prices running at the time. Stores sell different amounts (a tissue culture cup, a moss portion, a bunch of stems), so the price shown in the list is the cheapest in stock option rather than a like for like comparison. A price marked sold out is shown only when no listing found had stock. Plants with no price had no retail listing with a price; sorting by price puts them last.

    Growing conditions are typical ranges for submerged growth under strong light with injected CO2. Store pages linked under each plant show availability at the time of checking; stock changes often.
